What this model can tell you

Flight position and rocket orientation come from the same planar simulation. A normal force behind the center of gravity produces rotation; a 1 m rail constrains liftoff. Recovery opens 0.4 s after apogee, over 0.8 s.

Drag, aerodynamic coefficients, inertia, and canopy opening are estimates. The model omits roll, structural breakup, variable atmosphere, measured motor curves, and full 6-DOF aerodynamics. Exact zero-wind symmetry can retain a nose-up attitude during unrecovered descent.

For learning and comparisons, not launch or safety decisions. NASA: rocket stability.
